# Ann's taste profile and rating framework (inputs for phase 2)

## The 9-dimension framework

Built with Ann. It describes a book's profile; it does not predict enjoyment. Claude scores the dimensions; Ann's star rating sits alongside separately.

| Dimension | Scale |
| --- | --- |
| Ideas Density | 1-5 |
| Literary Register | 1-5 |
| Pacing | 1-5 |
| Thematic Weight | 1-5 |
| Structural Ambition | 1-5 |
| World-building | 1-5 |
| Emotional Register | 1 bleak to 5 warm |
| Warmth & Wit | 1-5 |
| Social Consciousness | 1 apolitical to 5 centrally socially conscious |

## What scores 5

- Ideas-first fiction: power structures, memory, consciousness, social architecture, civilisation, identity
- Structural ambition and purposeful pacing; form serving revelation
- Sharp, clear prose; dark wit; earned emotional warmth
- Strong social and political consciousness woven into the narrative
- Gothic and psychological fiction with structural payoff (du Maurier, Waters, Flynn)
- Warmth and substance together; Pratchett is the model. Never warmth at the expense of ideas.

## What scores low

- Stream-of-consciousness prose (Woolf 2s, Faulkner 2): a consistent negative signal
- Style or warmth prioritised over ideas (Mrs Dalloway, A Psalm for the Wild-Built, How to Stop Time)
- Predictability; memory/time themes handled without originality
- Sprawl without payoff: the sci-fi series decay pattern (Simmons 5 to 4)

## Recommendation principles

- Ann reads selectively and abandons books that aren't working, so low ratings are high-signal.
- Genre fatigue is real: she pivots rather than pushes through. The mood check exists for this.
- She has been shifting from sci-fi toward contemporary fiction.
- Never suggest a book she is currently reading.
- Audiobooks: hard ceiling of 10-12 hours.
